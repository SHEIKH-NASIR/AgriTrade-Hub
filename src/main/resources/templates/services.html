What We Offer

Explore the key features and services of AgriTrade Hub

🌾
Direct Farmer to Merchant Trading

Eliminate middlemen and promote fair pricing by connecting farmers directly with verified merchants.

📦
Live Product Listings

Farmers can list their available products in real-time, while merchants can browse and place orders anytime.

🛒
Easy Order Management

Track, manage and update order status with an intuitive dashboard designed for both farmers and merchants.

📁
Category-wise Product Filtering

Merchants can explore products based on dynamic categories to find exactly what they need quickly.

🔒
Secure Login & Profiles

Each user gets a secure login and personalized profile for viewing transactions, history, and product updates.

📈
Reports & Dashboards

Insightful dashboards for users to view their orders, revenue, and activity — updated in real-time.

💰
Fair Price Guard

Validate product prices against market reference prices and help maintain fair and transparent agricultural pricing.

🤖
AI Demand Forecasting

Use AI-based demand forecasting to provide procurement insights and help users make better purchasing decisions.

🚚
Smart Route & Logistics Optimization

Calculate practical delivery routes and optimize logistics using road distance and suitable vehicle selection.

🧾
Landed Cost Calculation

Show the complete cost of agricultural products by combining product price with applicable logistics costs for transparent purchasing decisions.

⚖️
Price + Logistics Comparison

Compare agricultural products using both product price and logistics cost instead of comparing product prices alone.

📦
Quantity & Bulk Purchase Management

Apply minimum and maximum quantity rules while supporting bulk purchasing for merchants and FPOs.
