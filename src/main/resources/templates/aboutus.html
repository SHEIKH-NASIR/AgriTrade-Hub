Who We Are

AgriTrade Hub is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to directly connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) with consumers and bulk buyers. Our platform aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries, improve farmer earnings, and provide consumers with transparent and competitive prices.

The platform combines digital marketplace services with logistics support, allowing buyers to understand the complete landed cost of agricultural products instead of comparing product prices alone.

AgriTrade Hub also uses technology-driven Fair Price validation, AI-based demand forecasting, and route/logistics optimization to create a more transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain.

🌱 Our Mission

To reduce intermediaries in agricultural trade by connecting farmers and FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers, helping farmers receive fair prices while enabling buyers to access quality products at transparent and competitive landed costs.

🚜 What We Offer

  • Direct Farmer, FPO and Consumer/Buyer Marketplace
  • Live Agricultural Product Listings and Category Filtering
  • Fair Price Guard with Market Price Validation
  • AI-Based Demand Forecasting and Procurement Insights
  • ORS-Based Logistics, Route Optimization and Landed Cost Calculation
  • Price + Logistics Comparison for Independent Farmers and FPOs
  • Online Orders, Razorpay Payments and Order Tracking
